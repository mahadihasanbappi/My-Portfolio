From Molecules to Markets

You might ask, "Why is a Pharmacist mastering Marketing?"

It started with science during my B.Pharm, understanding how medicines heal. But the plot twist happened during my time as a Medicine Information Associate (MIA). While promoting medicines to doctors, I realized a crucial truth: The best medicine is useless if the world doesn't understand its value.

That experience bridged the gap for me. I saw that Science builds the product, but Marketing builds the trust. Now, pursuing my MSc in Marketing, I combine my clinical knowledge with persuasive strategy to tell stories that save lives.
